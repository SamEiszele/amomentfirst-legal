Privacy Policy

A Moment First · Last updated 4 October 2026

The short version: A Moment First does not collect, sell, or share your personal data, usage data, or Screen Time information, and there's no account or sign-in. Almost everything the app knows about you stays on your device. The one exception: if you set shield hours, your device's notification token and that schedule (the times and days you chose) are sent to a server we operate, solely so it can nudge your device at the right time even if you haven't opened the app in a while — see "Shield-hours server reminders" below.

What the app can see, and where it stays

A Moment First uses Apple's Screen Time API (Family Controls) to let you choose which apps and websites it should give you a moment of pause before opening, and to enforce the shield hours you set. Apple's own privacy design means the app never learns the name of the apps or sites you choose — it only ever receives an anonymous, on-device reference to them, and that reference is never sent anywhere.

Your activity within the app — which moments you gained, your streak, your level, your settings, and anything else the app tracks — is stored using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults, shared only between A Moment First and its own system extensions via an App Group). None of this is transmitted anywhere. There's no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising software of any kind built into the app.

Shield-hours server reminders

If you turn on shield hours (the option to only block your chosen apps during certain times/days), the app sends two pieces of information to a server we operate: an opaque device notification token issued by Apple, and the schedule itself (the start/end times and days you set — never which apps or sites it applies to). The server uses this solely to trigger a silent, content-free notification at the right moment, so your shield hours can still start and stop reliably even if you haven't opened the app recently. This is not used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than that reminder, and is never sold or shared with anyone else. The stored record is overwritten every time your schedule changes, and updated to stop the reminders the moment you turn shield hours off. If the app isn't opened for 120 days, the record is deleted automatically.

Notifications

Every notification you actually see from A Moment First (level-ups, streak milestones, a moment to continue, shield-hours reminders) is composed and scheduled entirely on your device using Apple's local notification system — no notification content is ever sent to us or anyone else. The only thing our server ever sends your device is the silent, content-free ping described above, which carries no visible text of its own and exists only to prompt your device to check its own, already-on-device schedule.

Purchases

If you choose to purchase Full Bloom (monthly, annual, or lifetime), that purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. A Moment First never sees or stores your payment details — Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction. The app only ever checks with Apple whether you're entitled to Full Bloom; it doesn't send any information about you to make that check.

A weekly reflection, if you turn it on

If you enable the optional weekly reflection prompt, tapping "Write it" opens a draft in your own device's Mail app, pre-filled with a summary of your week. This draft is composed entirely on your device. A Moment First has no way to see whether you send it, edit it, or discard it, and nothing about it is transmitted back to us.

Children's privacy

A Moment First is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, of any age — the app collects nothing beyond the anonymous, non-identifying technical data described above, from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does involve data leaving your device — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.
